# PRODUCT

## The problem

A customer asks an AI assistant "where should I go for a romantic Italian dinner in Tel
Aviv?" and gets three names. If a business is not one of them, it has lost that customer
without ever knowing the conversation happened.

Traditional SEO tools measure rankings on a results page. That is no longer where the
decision is made.

## The promise

> Maximise your probability of being recommended by AI for relevant customer intents.

Not "guaranteed first in ChatGPT". The product does not control any external AI system and
says so on every surface that shows a number.

## The loop

```
USER INTENT → PROMPT → AI ANSWER → RECOMMENDATION → SOURCE → BUSINESS EVIDENCE
   → COMPETITOR GAP → CONTROLLABLE GAP → OPTIMIZATION → EXPERIMENT → MEASUREMENT
   → LEARNING → NEXT OPTIMIZATION
```

## What makes it defensible

**The Evidence Graph.** Not "you should write more content", but: *37 monitored questions
carry the date-night intent; you are recommended in 4% of them and Vito in 31%; your own
site never states the attribute your confirmed business data supports; that gap is
CONTROLLED.* Every step of that sentence is a row in a table.

**Honesty as a feature.** The product tells customers what it cannot do. A competitor's
advantage that rests on independent editorial coverage is labelled an external authority gap,
and the product says plainly that it cannot manufacture reputation. That is a harder sale in
week one and a much easier renewal in month six.

**The learning dataset.** Prompts, intents, observed recommendations, entities, attributes,
sources, competitor states, interventions and *experiment outcomes*, schema'd from day one.
The eventual question — *for this category, prompt class, geography and attribute, which
interventions actually move recommendation probability?* — becomes a query against data
already owned. That is the moat, not the content generator.

## Customer language

The product never says "your semantic entity graph is under-optimized". It says:

> AI understands that you are an Italian restaurant, but it does not consistently associate
> you with date-night dining.

Every opportunity, every finding and every agent action is written this way, and the tests
assert the absence of jargon.

## Onboarding

Target: value in under 10 minutes, first signal in under 2.

1. Enter a website
2. Immediate technical audit and provisional entity — something on screen in seconds
3. Business facts confirmed by the owner (this gates everything the product may later claim)
4. Prompt universe generated in Hebrew and English
5. First measurement and AIRS
6. Top opportunities
7. Optional integrations
8. Choose an automation level — new tenants start at RECOMMEND

## Pricing

₪699/month + VAT (Growth). Annual ₪6,990 + VAT. A free diagnostic scan is the top of the
funnel, capped so it cannot run up a real bill.

## What it will not do

Scrape consumer chat interfaces. Fabricate reviews, citations, awards or testimonials.
Generate doorway pages or mass thin content. Hide text or embed instructions aimed at answer
engines. Promise a ranking. Publish a claim the business has not confirmed.

Each of those would work for a while, and each would eventually destroy both the customer's
business and this one.
